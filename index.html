JS Health Initiative East Africa is a non-profit organisation working to support underprivileged communities through digital skilling, public health education, research, and surgical care support.

About us

Registered in Uganda as a company limited by guarantee, JS Health Initiative East Africa is on the path to full PNFP/NGO registration. We believe access to health, knowledge, and skills should not depend on where a person is born.
