# Impact story template (consent first)

Use this for every story, photo or quote. Do not publish until every box is ticked.

## Consent checklist
- [ ] Purpose explained in the person's own language (and to a parent/guardian if under 18)
- [ ] Written or recorded consent saved privately, with the date
- [ ] Person chose: full name / first name only / anonymous
- [ ] Person reviewed the final text and photos and approved them
- [ ] Told they can withdraw at any time, and how (contact.html or email)
- [ ] No health detail published beyond what they agreed to
- [ ] No payment or gift given in exchange for the story (cover transport/time only, openly)

## Structure (150 to 250 words)
**Headline:** a decision or a change, not a hardship. Example: "[Name] now teaches [skill] to [group]".
**Who and where:** [First name or chosen name], [age or role], [village/parish], Western Uganda.
**Before:** one or two sentences in their words about the situation, without pity language.
**What happened:** what [Name] decided, who helped (family, local clinic, our team), what we did and did not do.
**What changed:** one concrete, verifiable change, with timing ("since [month]").
**What is still hard:** one honest sentence.
**In their words:** one short quote they approved.
**How you can help:** one specific action: [donate / volunteer / partner link].
**Footer line:** "Shared with [Name]'s consent on [date]. Photos by [name]."

## Words to avoid
"Rescued", "helpless", "beneficiary", "lucky", "the poor", "saved". Prefer the person's name, their role and their actions.

## Replace the homepage section
In index.html, replace the content inside `<section id="stories">` with a story card using the headline, three sentences, the approved quote and a link to the full story.
